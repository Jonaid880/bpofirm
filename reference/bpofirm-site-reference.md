- **Domain:** https://bpofirm.com/

  - 99.9% accuracy claim
  - Cuts costs by **30–70%**
  - Reduces operational overhead by **60%**
  - Fully trained, dedicated team operational within **7 business days**
  - 24/7 operational resilience
  - **GDPR & HIPAA compliant**; strict access controls, data encryption, confidentiality protocols
